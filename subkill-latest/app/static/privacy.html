SubKill Privacy Policy

SubKill helps you find and cancel subscriptions. This explains what we handle and why. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Subscription details: for each subscription found or added by you: merchant, plan, amount, billing cycle, dates, category, who in your household it belongs to, and your cancellation progress.
  • Text you submit: emails, receipts, bank statement lines, or chat messages. Pasted or uploaded source text is used to extract subscriptions and is not stored afterwards. Chat messages are sent to the AI to answer you and are not kept on our servers once the conversation ends.

How it is used

Text you submit and your subscription list are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to extract subscriptions, answer your questions, and prepare cancellation steps. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms and API data policies. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Cancellations

SubKill never logs in to other companies' accounts and never sends email on your behalf unless you review and send it yourself. You complete every cancellation.

Security and retention

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored on our server until you delete it. Session tokens are kept in your device's Keychain.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the menu and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all subscription data from our server.
